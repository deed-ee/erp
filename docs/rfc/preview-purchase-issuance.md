# Issue and receive local NovaMotion purchase orders

**Design for the confirmed [#23 preview direction](https://github.com/erp4us-org/erp4us/issues/23#issuecomment-7237683084), not implementation evidence.** This is a synthetic, local purchase record and physical receipt workflow; there is no supplier transmission, email, payment, or budget-balance reservation. The [budget approval design](preview-budget-approvals.md) precedes issuance.

## Procurement owns issuance

Propose an explicit, separately granted command, `POST /v1/procurement/proposals/by-plan/{plan_id}/purchase-orders`, under `procurement.purchase-order.issue`. The verified actor may not be the preparer of the reviewed terms. It names a nonzero `command_id` and the exact approved terms and policy identifiers and revisions; it cannot supply prices, quantities, recipients, actors, or authority. Recompute the current approval state from valid persisted decisions against the entire EUR draft, before grouping by supplier. The command must reject absent, incompatible or stale policy, missing stage, stale terms, or wrong scope. A prior approval or browser control is not authority to issue.

Persist one immutable **issuance batch per scoped plan**. Its child purchase order for each verified supplier holds the original supplier snapshot, positive immutable `EA` lines, exact quantity, exact price and amount, assigned order/line IDs, draft version, actor and creation time. Child totals must sum exactly to the approved draft total. Grouping does not change the budget result; splitting a draft across suppliers is not a way to satisfy a lower threshold. The batch and its idempotency receipt are one local Procurement transaction. Use the existing scoped write guard shared with terms edits and approvals: reread the exact latest revision and complete decisions after acquiring it, and reject edits once the batch exists. An original authorized retry returns the original IDs, while a second action or changed intent cannot issue the plan again. Corrections/cancellations are separate later work, not destructive changes to an issued record.

Illustrative preview: the saved five-line NovaMotion purchase draft totals `1882.40` EUR. If all five terms use the same supplier, an explicit authorized issue creates one purchase order with five lines, not five orders. If two suppliers were approved on the draft, it creates two child orders but still uses the same approved total. A fully covered reservation has no shortage proposal and never enters this path.

## Inventory owns receipt and allocation

The receiver works against an issued, immutable Procurement line, not a draft or client-provided proof of purchase. Define a language-neutral owner contract before wiring it. A first approach is a narrow Procurement GET for the scoped issued receipt snapshot (order/line IDs, plan, item/revision, authorized whole ordered quantity), using the receiver's verified actor and scope, independent from shipment/price or purchase authority. Unavailable verification fails without changing Inventory. If a later version uses a durable issued fact instead, it must have a stable owner outbox event and exact scope, explicit stream/consumer compatibility and independently enforced inbox deduplication.

Inventory's distinct receipt grant authorizes a whole-piece receipt command with a stable `receipt_id` and quantity against that issued line. Persist received total, stock movement, allocation to the original waiting plan, and the receipt fact/outbox atomically in Inventory. Retrying the same scoped receipt and quantity cannot allocate twice; changing it under that ID, racing past the line balance, or posting against an unissued or foreign line fails. Procurement derives received/open status from the committed Inventory fact, not the browser, expected delivery or an update from another service's database. A receipt is distinct from a purchase authorization, invoice and shipment.

**End plate acceptance:** the issued order is for 12 and the plan initially holds 8. Receive 6: held 14, open 6. Replay the same receipt: no changes. Receive 6 under a new ID: held 20, open 0. A thirteenth plate must be rejected. Test two competing partial receipts, wrong scope/grant, broker and owning-service outages, restart and explicit reconciliation. Also test that issuance has no stock effect and a receipt cannot be sent before the order exists.

**Scope:** preserve actor, tenant and organization throughout; use exact money and quantities. No automatic issue, self-approval, over-receipt, buyer/receiver privilege inference, supplier transmission, receipt corrections, or full acceptance claim. The narrower issuing operation/API is independent of the physical receipt work and can be tested first.
