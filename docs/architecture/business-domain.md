# Cross-industry business domain model

This is the target domain model, not an implemented schema or a deployment plan. The local Article API is the only implemented business slice. See the [catalog](../requirements/README.md) for milestones and evidence, and the [boundary review](cross-industry-review.md) for migration gates.

## Tenant, organization and authority

A tenant is the isolation boundary, not a company. Within it, model legal (corporate groups, legal entities and legal responsibility), operational (companies, business units, departments, sites, factories, construction sites, teams) and reporting (cost/profit centers) dimensions separately. Designations can overlap without implying the same hierarchy or granting access; Inventory owns warehouse/storage identities and Production owns work centers, with references to organizational scope. Model shared master records and scoped local records or overrides explicitly. Posting and intercompany transactions identify each legal party and retain local snapshots; future Finance owns consolidation and elimination, not a cross-tenant join.

Organizations owns memberships and scope policy, and provides organization context for language, currency, tax jurisdiction, time zone and calendar. Services authorize commands against the resolved actor, tenant, organization and resource. “Shared within tenant” is an explicit policy, not an absent filter. User, service and agent calls have the same enforcement.

## Identity, master data and characteristics

Master Data owns stable partner and product definitions, contacts, addresses, shared unit and classification catalogs, and governed identifiers. Customer, supplier, manufacturer, carrier and service-provider roles reference one partner. Products cover raw materials, semifinished/finished goods, assemblies, consumables, services and digital goods. A product can combine purchasing, selling, manufacturing, stock, configuration, serialization, batch tracking, expiration, hazard, maintenance, rental, recycling and digital delivery capabilities; none implies another. A capability defines its typed payload, version, permissions, activation constraints and owning behavior. For example, Master Data owns the identity, Inventory owns stock and lot state, Sales owns offers and prices, and Service manages maintenance execution. Domain roles and capabilities can refer to service-owned details without copying their authority.

Other master records belong with their invariants: Assets (machines, tools, vehicles and assets), Workforce (employees and qualifications), Organizations or owning contexts (locations and calendars), Inventory (warehouses and bins), commercial contexts (price lists and contracts), Documents (document metadata and private S3 binaries). These are candidate responsibilities, not a requirement for a deployment per record type. Classifications can be many-to-many; identity is separate from all business numbers. Identifier assignments carry scheme, issuing authority, normalized value and uniqueness scope with scheme-specific validation. Receipts, orders and other historical facts refer to stable IDs and retain the meaning in force (revision or explicit snapshot). Plan numbers, revisions, effective dates, lifecycle and approvals, import/export, quality checks, duplicate detection and audited merge with redirects that preserve old references.

A versioned attribute definition specifies value type, cardinality, validation, defaults, requiredness, applicability, units, labels and access. Support text/localized text, integer, exact decimal, boolean, date and time, enum, multiselect, typed references, measurements, schema-validated structured values and ranges. Classification groups and hierarchies compose approved definitions; reject cycles, incompatible overrides and ambiguous inheritance before activation. Industry definitions (e.g. wood species, moisture, dimensions, concentration, CAS identifiers, grades, construction codes and electrical ratings) are namespaced, not core fields. Query and filter via typed, permitted fields with deliberate indexes/projections; apply the same rules to APIs, search, exports and agents. MongoDB's document flexibility is not permission to accept arbitrary keys or unvalidated logic.

## Locale and translations

System and module strings use resource keys and standard BCP 47 locale tags. Master data, attribute labels and values, documents and user-generated content have owner-scoped translatable resources with source locale and source revision. A translation records the target locale, source revision, origin, provider (if applicable), status, review state, timestamps and translator/reviewer identity. A source change marks translations stale without deleting approved text; provider output is a candidate and never silently replaces an approved human translation. If no approved current text is available, source fallback keeps the business operation functional and the UI can signal stale or missing content.

Translation Service coordinates optional manual, external API, OpenAI-compatible, local and hosted model adapters, translation memory, glossaries/domain terminology, batch jobs and missing/stale detection. The owning service authorizes retrieval and application against the source revision; configured workflows decide human review and approval. Default-deny outbound transfer, authorize the provider and classified fields for the tenant/purpose before any request, and isolate memory, cache and jobs by authorized scope. Versioned module manifests may register resource types, not grant data access. No provider or AI is required.

## Quantities, structures and operations

Represent quantities as exact decimals with explicit units and dimensions (count, length, area, volume, mass, time, energy, temperature, concentration, packages and industry units). Distinguish dimensional conversions (including affine ones) from product- or context-specific conversions such as variable weight, moisture and packaging. Preserve original readings and simultaneous quantities, actual vs. nominal, tolerance, applied formula/factor version, parameters and rounding. A case-to-unit ratio is not globally valid; the as-measured quantity is not replaced by a conversion.

Product structures are typed, versioned and effective-dated definitions: engineering, manufacturing, sales, service and configurable BOMs, recipes/formulas, transformations, bills of quantities and packaging trees. Support multiple levels, alternatives/substitutes, variants/options, formula quantities, scrap/yield, co-products/by-products, release and engineering change approval. Release effective revisions, validate references and allowed cycles by structure type, and pin the relevant revisions when planning or executing.

Production separates definitions (operations, routings, inputs, outputs, resources/work centers, labor, setup/run times, capacity, parameters, checks and planned costs) from execution (actual parameters, labor, usage, output, quality and costs). Plan discrete, batch, process, continuous and project work, including assembly, disassembly, transformation, rework, recycling and subcontracting. Make-to-stock, make-to-order, assemble-to-order and engineer-to-order are planning strategies, not entity classes. A transformation has independent input and output collections with explicit roles; an operation need not match a whole product structure. Do not equate planned yield with measured results.

## Traceability, quality and lifecycle views

Identification schemes can address product, serial, lot/batch, asset, handling unit, package, project and external references, with barcode, QR and RFID encodings. Configurable number sequences allocate business identifiers in the configured scope; they do not allocate entity identity. A typed, tenant-scoped reference describes owner, resource type and stable ID (plus revision when needed). Cross-service discovery uses documented APIs and event-built read models. Filter both ends and the relation for authorization before returning a link, an aggregate count or evidence from a traversal.

Procurement, Inventory, Production and Service persist their actual quantities and lineage evidence where they author a business fact. Cover receiving, storage, assembly, splitting, merging, mixing, transformation, repack, rework, shipment, service and recycling. A forward/backward projection connects typed inputs, outputs and movements to support authorized impact and customer recall searches, preserving source and revision. It is rebuildable from retained authoritative evidence, not from temporary JetStream retention alone. Corrections link to prior facts instead of deleting history.

Quality owns inspection plans, incoming/in-process/final inspections and measurements, certificates, deviations, nonconformities, corrective/preventive actions, supplier quality, complaints and recall coordination. Stock availability must honor structured quarantine/release rules at the Inventory owner. Modules supply jurisdiction- and industry-specific rules (hazard information, safety data sheets, expiry, food traceability, pharmaceutical validation); features do not establish regulatory compliance without independent validation.

A **Digital Product Record** composes authorized views of product definitions, revisions, variants, physical instances, batches, assets, installed and customer-owned products. Keep as-designed, as-planned, as-built, as-delivered, as-maintained and as-retired evidence separate and show source, revision and freshness. Compose specifications and structures, serial/batch/lineage, production and quality results, certificates/drawings/documents and change history, software/firmware, customer and ownership relationships, installation, warranty/service/maintenance, spare parts, costs, sustainability and end-of-life information. Each service remains authoritative for its facts; do not dump all data into a product document. A later Digital Product Passport is a versioned disclosure subset with its own audience/policy, not a public copy of the record.

## Configuration and packages

Use versioned, independently publishable Industry Packages for classifiers, attributes, units and typed capabilities, process/workflow and document templates, inspection plans, dashboards/reports, localization, services and adapters. Multiple packages may be active for one tenant or company. Registry/operator validates compatibility, dependencies, publisher, permissions, conflicts and migration/rollback before activation; packages cannot claim ownership of other services' data.

Configurable lifecycle transitions, approvals, numbering, templates, typed rules, responsibilities, required attributes, quality gates and notifications call vetted service commands. Persist the applied policy version for historical records. A configuration may add conditions, not bypass authorization, mandatory invariants, accounting integrity or traceability. Start with fixed deterministic rules; introduce typed extension points only as use cases justify them.
